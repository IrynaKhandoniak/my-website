Habits for Life — highlights

Good daily habits

  • ❤️ Give love freely — share kindness without expecting anything back.
  • 🏋️ Move your body — be active and your body and mind will thank you.
  • 🥗 Eat with care — nourish yourself, drink water, listen to your body.
  • 👋 Connect with others — meet new people or call someone you enjoy.
  • 🛠 Create & express — build, code, write, sing, paint something meaningful.
  • 🧘 Pause & breathe — 10 minutes to meditate and let your mind settle.
  • 📖 Keep a journal — practice gratitude and note your intentions.
  • 🕺 Learn & grow — explore hobbies, sharpen skills, stay curious.
  • 📜 Revisit wisdom — return to quotes, books, and talks that inspire.
  • 🍷 Slow down — savour music, good food, laughter, and quiet moments.

Habits to avoid

  • 😍 Scattering your focus — don't chase too many desires at once.
  • 💊 Relying on substances — avoid using alcohol or pills as coping tools.
  • 📰 Doomscrolling the news — protect your peace, most of it is noise.
  • 🏨 Luxury obsession — keep it simple; happiness isn't found in excess.
  • 👍 Craving approval — your worth isn't measured by likes or comments.
  • 📅 Overloading your schedule — leave space to breathe and recharge.
  • 😠 Complaining without action — accept what is, then improve it.
  • 🦠 Toxic environments — protect your energy from draining spaces.
  • 😪 Harmful relationships — choose supportive, positive people.
